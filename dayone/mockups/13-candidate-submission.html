1 · What did you find, and how confident are you?
The latency regression came from the v2.14.0 retry loop holding a pooled DB connection and an open transaction while sleeping in backoff. Under partial gateway degradation ~7 req/s was enough to saturate a 20-connection pool. High confidence: reproduced with the load test, confirmed pool saturation in metrics, and p95 dropped from 1.74 s to 310 ms after releasing the connection before backoff.
