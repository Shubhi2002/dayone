Why a shared worldThe candidate learns one codebase once. Rounds 2 and 3 reuse it, so orientation cost is paid a single time and transitions between modes become part of the signal.
Why independent roundsEach round starts from a canonical state ("the team finished it overnight"), so a weak round 1 cannot sink rounds 2 and 3, and each mode's score stays attributable.
Where AI collaboration is scoredEverywhere. Every round has the agent; prompts, rejections, verifications and blind accepts are extracted per round and rolled up.
What the company never seesVideo, keystroke biometrics, demographic data, or an integrity verdict. They see evidence, agreement rates and a recommendation they can dispute.
