Eight dimensions, each traceable to moments in the replay. Grader agreement shown. Human review on a sample and on every dispute.

Process is the signal

Two candidates can submit the same diff and be completely different engineers.

We score how the work was done, not only whether it was done. Every signal below is computed from the session trace, never self-reported.

Trust, without surveillance

Integrity comes from scenario design and evidence, not from a webcam.

No video, ever

We record edits, commands, tests and prompts inside the sandbox. No webcam, no gaze tracking, no keystroke biometrics.

Variants, not question banks

Every session is a materially different instance of the scenario. A leaked solution does not transfer, and the rubric scores the process anyway.

Humans stay in the loop

Five independent graders per rubric item, a human review on a sample and on every disagreement, and a dispute path open to companies and candidates.

Built for fairness law

Job-related work samples, candidate notice, adverse-impact monitoring, accommodations without justification. Designed for EEOC, NYC LL144 and the EU AI Act.
