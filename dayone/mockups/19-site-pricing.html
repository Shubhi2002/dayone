Questions

Why not price per seat?

Usage tracks value delivered and hiring is lumpy. The platform fee keeps the library maintained; assessments are what you actually consume.

How is this cheaper than Saffron's $199?

It isn't, and it isn't trying to be. Their assessment is a feature-building task. Ours is a multi-round simulation with telemetry, events and human calibration, replacing a 60 to 90 minute engineer-led round.

What happens to candidate data?

We process it on your behalf under a DPA. Default retention is 12 months, configurable. Candidates can request access or deletion at any time, and receive their own report after your decision.

Can we bring our own codebase?

On Growth and above. We fork a scenario onto your repo, adapt the rubric to your conventions, and build the sandbox from your Dockerfile. Your code never leaves your isolated tenancy and is never used for training.
